Checkout Mode

Add items, review, done.

Scan a barcode or search by SKU to add items to the cart. Adjust quantities, see the running total with GST calculated automatically, then choose to save, share, or print — in one tap.

Scan or type. Use a barcode scanner, your device camera, or just type a SKU or product name.

Review before committing. See the full cart with per-item totals and GST before saving anything.

Three ways to finish. Save to records, save and share digitally, or save and print on the spot.

Stock updates automatically. Every checkout deducts from your inventory in real time — no double entry.

Custom Invoicing

Professional invoices, straight from the app.

Every checkout can generate a branded invoice with your company name and logo. Invoices are automatically saved to a folder of your choice — organised, searchable, and ready when you need them.

Add a job number. Attach a job or project number to any invoice so you can reconcile purchases against specific jobs later.

Saved to your folder. Every invoice is stored automatically — browse your full receipts history any time from within the app.

Print or share. Send the invoice digitally or print it on the spot using any connected printer — including receipt printers.

Your branding. Add your company name and logo so every invoice looks professional and on-brand.

Everything in one place

Built for the counter, the stall, and the stockroom.

🖨️

Print on the spot

Connect any printer — standard or receipt — and print invoices or receipts the moment a transaction is complete. No exporting, no extra steps.

📁

Receipts folder

Every invoice and receipt is saved automatically to a folder you choose. Your full transaction history is always at your fingertips.

Job number tracking

Tag any transaction with a job number so you can reconcile stock usage against specific projects, clients, or events.

Live inventory sync

Every item sold deducts from your inventory in real time. No separate stock count needed — the checkout and the stockroom stay in perfect sync.

Save & share

Send invoices digitally via email, AirDrop, or any sharing method your device supports — straight from the checkout screen.

🏢

Company details

Set your business name, logo, and contact details once — they appear automatically on every invoice you generate.
