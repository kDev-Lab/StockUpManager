Problem Solved

Right now, your stockroom is a black hole.

Every "I thought we had it in stock" moment costs downtime, a scramble to substitute items, and sometimes a delayed job. It isn't a people problem — it's a visibility problem. Stock goes missing, nobody writes it down, and the first anyone hears about it is when someone reaches for it and it's gone.

Invisible

  • No one knows what's on the shelf until it's too late.
  • Stock gets grabbed with no record of who took it, or when.
  • Reordering happens in a panic, often with rush shipping fees.
  • Low stock is only noticed after the job has already stalled.

Visible

  • Every scan updates the shelf count instantly, for everyone.
  • Every deduction has a name and a timestamp attached.
  • Low-stock alerts fire before the shelf actually runs dry.
  • Reordering happens on your schedule — not in a panic.

Sound familiar?

A critical item runs out mid-job and everything grinds to a halt.

Low-stock alerts fire before the shelf runs dry, so a replacement is already on the way.

Sound familiar?

Nobody remembers who took the last of a key item.

Every deduction is signed with a PIN, so it's always clear who took what, and when.

Sound familiar?

A team gets restocked with the wrong supplies, again.

One shared, live count means everyone's working from the same numbers — no more guessing.

"Right now, your inventory is invisible. Stock Up Manager makes it visible."
Go one step further: alert your suppliers automatically. Low-stock alerts don't have to stop at your team. If required, they can be routed straight to your suppliers too, so replacement stock is already on its way before anyone's even noticed the gap on the shelf.
Job numbers on every deduction. In Checkout Mode, staff can tag each deduction with a job number, so stock use is tracked against the job it went to — not just the person who scanned it.
